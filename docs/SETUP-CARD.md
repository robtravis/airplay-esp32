# Vibe Radio

A little radio that plays Vibe Radio the moment you plug it in — and doubles as an
AirPlay speaker for your phone or laptop.

---

## Setting it up (once)

**1. Plug it in.** Any USB power supply. The screen shows **VIBE RADIO**, then
**Join WiFi**.

**2. On your phone, join the WiFi network shown on the screen** —
`Vibe Radio Setup`. A setup page should open by itself. If it doesn't, open a
browser and go to **192.168.4.1**.

**3. Pick your home WiFi, type the password, tap Connect.**

That's it. Within a few seconds the screen shows the station and the music starts.
It remembers your WiFi, so from now on it just plays when you plug it in.

---

## Using it

**The knob does three things:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Turn** | Volume |
| **Press** | Mute / unmute |
| **Hold** (about a second) | Open the menu |

**In the menu**, turn to move, press to choose, hold to go back.

- **Archive Shows** — past shows, pick one and it plays. It moves through the
  episodes on its own, then returns to the live station.
- **Visualizer** — the spectrum display along the bottom, on or off
- **LED FX** — eight looks for the light ring
- **Brightness** — for the ring
- **Network** — its address on your network
- **Info** — name, volume, current effect

**To play your own music:** pick **Vibe Radio** from the AirPlay menu on your
phone, Mac or iPad. The screen switches to AIRPLAY and shows what's playing. When
you stop, the station comes back a few seconds later.

---

## Two things worth knowing

**If you ever unplug it, plug it back in and wait a moment.** It always needs a
proper power cycle rather than a quick tap of the cable.

**If you change your WiFi or move house**, hold the knob and go to
**Network → Forget Network**. It reopens its own setup network and shows you what
to join, exactly like the first time. No computer needed.

---

## If something seems wrong

**Screen on, no sound** — check it isn't muted (press the knob), and that the
volume isn't turned down.

**Stuck on "Join WiFi"** — it can't reach your network. Join
`Vibe Radio Setup` and enter the details again. Double-check the password.

**Nothing on the screen at all** — unplug it, wait five seconds, plug it back in.

**It plays but stutters** — usually weak WiFi. Move it closer to the router. If
that helps but isn't enough, turn the Visualizer off in the menu.
